What the records teach

  • Shortness wins. Single characters (“1”, “W”, “P 7”) top every market.
  • 8 is the premium digit in Chinese markets: 28, 18 and 88 are all HK$11M+ plates.
  • Charity auctions set records. Dubai's biggest sales came from Most Noble Numbers.
  • Phone numbers are licences, not property — even the AED 7.82M Etisalat number was a service contract.

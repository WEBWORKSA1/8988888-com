Frequently asked

What does 8988888 mean?
Read in Mandarin it is bā-jiǔ-bā-bā-bā-bā-bā. 8 (bā) rhymes with 发 fā, 'to prosper', and 9 (jiǔ) sounds like 久, 'long-lasting'. Together the string reads as 'prosperity that lasts, then fortune five times over'. It contains no 4, the number Chinese buyers avoid.
Can I buy a lucky phone number?
It depends on the country. In China and the UAE, carriers and authorised auctions sell premium numbers. In the US and Canada, toll-free numbers cannot legally be bought or sold (FCC 47 CFR §52.107); you reserve them through a Responsible Organization. Local numbers move with a business or by porting. Our concierge works only through legitimate channels.
How accurate is the value estimator?
It scores patterns that auction data shows buyers pay for: repeats, few distinct digits, ladders, mirrors, 8s, no 4s and shortness. It returns an indicative band, not an appraisal. For a real valuation, use the free valuation request.
Is this site affiliated with any auction house or carrier?
No. 8988888.com is an independent information and lead-generation site. Records link to their original public sources.

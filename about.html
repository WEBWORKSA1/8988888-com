About 8988888

An independent reference hub and concierge for lucky, vanity and premium numbers.

Our mission

Numbers carry meaning, and people pay for meaning. We make that market transparent: free tools that explain what a number is worth and why, records that cite their sources, guides that explain the rules, and a concierge that only works through legitimate channels.

What we stand for

  • Sourced, not hyped. Every record links to its source.
  • Legal routes only. We don't broker toll-free numbers or bypass carrier and registry rules.
  • Privacy first. Tools run in your browser.
  • Respect for culture. Number beliefs are traditions, not guarantees — we explain them, we don't sell luck.

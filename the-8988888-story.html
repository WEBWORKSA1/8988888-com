How to read 8988888

In Mandarin the digits are read one at a time: bā jiǔ bā bā bā bā bā. Chinese number culture runs on homophones — numbers that sound like words — and two of the luckiest homophones in the language are packed into this one string:

  • 8 · 八 bā rhymes with 发 fā, as in 发财 fācái, “to get rich”. In Cantonese, 八 baat and 發 faat are closer still.
  • 9 · 九 jiǔ is pronounced exactly like 久 jiǔ, “long-lasting”.

So “89” reads as 发久 — prosperity that lasts — and the five 8s that follow add fortune upon fortune: 发久发发发发发. That is the meaning behind our tagline, lasting prosperity.

Eight: the prosperity digit

Eight is the single most valuable digit in Chinese-speaking markets, and the evidence is public:

  • Beijing opened the 2008 Summer Olympics on 8 August 2008 at 8:08 pm.
  • Sichuan Airlines paid ¥2.33 million (about US$280,700) for the phone number 8888-8888 in Chengdu in 2003. That single auction of 100 numbers raised ¥7.13 million.
  • A court-seized Beijing mobile number with five consecutive 8s drew 5,000+ bids and sold for ¥2.25 million (about US$300,000) in 2020.
  • Hong Kong plates 28 (HK$18.1M), 18 (HK$16.5M) and 88 (HK$11.4M) are among the most expensive two-digit marks ever sold.
  • Dubai's 054-8888888 fetched AED 2.3 million at a 2023 charity auction.

Nine: the longevity digit

Nine is the highest single digit and, through 久, the digit of permanence. It was long associated with the emperor: imperial architecture is full of nines (nine-dragon walls, door studs in rows of nine), and the Double Ninth Festival (重阳节, the 9th day of the 9th lunar month) is a day for honouring elders. Couples favour 9 for weddings — 久久 (99) means “forever”. Placed after an 8, it turns a burst of luck into lasting luck.

Five eights and no four

The number also wins by what it leaves out. 4 · 四 sì sounds like 死 sǐ, “death”. Many buildings in China, Hong Kong and Singapore skip 4th, 14th and 24th floors, and published research shows the digit measurably discounts prices (see below). 8988888 has no 4, only two distinct digits, and a run of five identical digits — exactly the patterns our value estimator rewards.

8 and 9 around the world

India & Hinduism

Ashta Lakshmi — the eight forms of Lakshmi, goddess of wealth. Navaratri is nine nights; the Navagraha are nine celestial influences.

Japan

八 is “suehirogari” — widening like a fan, so growing prosperity. 9 (ku) can sound like 苦, “suffering”, so it is avoided in some contexts.

Buddhism

The Noble Eightfold Path and the eight auspicious symbols (Ashtamangala).

Judaism & Christianity

Hanukkah lasts eight days; eight often symbolises new beginnings and resurrection.

Mathematics & the West

Nine worlds on Yggdrasil; nine Muses in Greek myth — nine as completeness.

The economy of a lucky number

Lucky numbers are not just folklore — they move money, and economists have measured it:

  • Housing: Fortin, Hill & Huang (2014) studied 116,939 Greater Vancouver sales from 2000–2005. Addresses ending in 8 sold at a 2.5% premium; those ending in 4 at a 2.2% discount, concentrated in neighbourhoods with larger Chinese populations.
  • Price pointing: listings at $888,888 are designed to attract Chinese buyers (UBC, 2016).
  • Plates: Ng, Chong & Du (2010) analysed Hong Kong auctions from 1997–2009: replacing a 4 with an 8 on a three-digit plate raised the price by 134.8% before 2006 and 97.4% after.
  • Phone numbers: carriers in China and the UAE auction premium numbers; Etisalat's 050-7777777 drew AED 7.82M in 2014.
  • Charity: Dubai's Most Noble Numbers auctions turn plates and numbers into eight-figure donations — plate P 7 raised AED 55M in 2023.

People and diaspora markets

The numbers matter wherever Chinese-speaking communities live and shop — Greater China, Southeast Asia, and the large diaspora communities of Vancouver, Toronto, Sydney, London, New York and the San Francisco Bay Area. Retailers time launches to 8/8, developers price units with trailing 8s, and families plan weddings on dates heavy with 8s and 9s. In the Gulf and India, premium numbers carry status in their own right — which is why numbers like 8988888 are a cross-cultural asset.

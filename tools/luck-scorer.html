FAQ

Is the luck score scientific?
It quantifies a cultural tradition consistently. It doesn't predict outcomes — but it does predict how many Chinese-speaking buyers will feel about a number.
What's the luckiest number?
Strings of 8s are the classic answer; 8888 means prosperity in all four seasons. 8988888 combines 8s with 9 for lasting prosperity.
Do Cantonese and Mandarin meanings differ?
Sometimes. 2 and 3 are luckier in Cantonese (易 easy, 生 life); the core meanings of 4, 8 and 9 are shared.

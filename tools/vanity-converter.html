Why vanity numbers work

A number that spells a word is easier to remember than seven random digits — that's why brands put 1-800-FLOWERS or 1-800-CONTACTS on billboards and radio. A memorable number lifts recall in any medium where people can't click.

The rules in North America

Toll-free numbers (800, 833, 844, 855, 866, 877, 888) are reserved through a Responsible Organization (RespOrg). They can't be hoarded or sold for a fee (FCC 47 CFR §52.107). If the word you want is taken, try a different prefix, a shorter word, or a local vanity number. Read the full toll-free guide.

FAQ

What digits have no letters?
1 and 0 carry no letters, so words can't include them.
Can my word be longer than 7 letters?
Yes — callers dial the extra letters but the network ignores them after the 7th digit of a North American number.
How do I check if a vanity number is available?
Send it to our concierge; we check availability with licensed providers.

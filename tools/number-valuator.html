Score the rarity of any mobile number, plate or numeric domain and see the indicative market band buyers have paid for similar patterns.

How rarity drives price

Across Hong Kong, Dubai and UK auctions, the same patterns command premiums again and again: shortness, repeats, few distinct digits, mirrors and ladders, a high share of 8s and no 4s. The estimator scores each of these and maps the total to a tier. See the full methodology and the Hall of Records.

FAQ

Is this an appraisal?
No. It's a pattern-based indicator. For a human-reviewed range, request a free valuation.
Why does 4 lower the score?
4 (sì) sounds like death (死) in Chinese. Research on Hong Kong plates and Vancouver homes shows measurable discounts for 4s.
Can I value toll-free numbers?
We show a memorability score, but US/Canadian toll-free numbers can't legally be sold, so there's no market price.

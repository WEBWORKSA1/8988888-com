What makes a plate valuable

  • Shortness: single characters (“1”, “W”, “P 7”) set world records.
  • Lucky digits: in Hong Kong, 28, 18 and 88 have each sold for over HK$11M.
  • Readable words or initials: the UK market prizes plates that spell names.

FAQ

Can I buy any plate I design?
Only if the issuing authority allows that format and it's available — e.g. HK personalised marks (up to 8 characters) or UK DVLA formats.
Do plates count as investments?
Some collectors treat them that way, but prices are volatile and illiquid. This isn't financial advice.

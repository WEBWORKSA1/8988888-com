🧧 Red Envelope (Hongbao) Calculator

A lucky, etiquette-aware amount for weddings, Lunar New Year, birthdays, babies and business openings — in eight currencies.

The golden rules

  • Even amounts for happy occasions (odd amounts are for funerals).
  • Never 4 — no $40, $400 or $444.
  • 8s and 6s are ideal; 520 and 1314 (“I love you”, “a lifetime”) suit weddings in Mainland China.
  • New, crisp notes, and hand the envelope over with both hands.

Lunar New Year 2027 begins on 6 February 2027 (Year of the Goat).

FAQ

How much for a wedding red envelope?
Many guests aim to at least cover their seat at the banquet, then round to a lucky amount; closer relationships give more.
Can I send a digital red envelope?
Yes — WeChat and Alipay red packets are common in Mainland China; the same number rules apply.

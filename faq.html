Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about lucky numbers, the rules, our tools and our services.

What is 8988888.com?
An independent hub for lucky, vanity and premium numbers: free tools, sourced records, guides, and a concierge for buying, valuing and listing numbers, plates and numeric domains.
Can I buy a 1-800 number from you?
No one can legally sell you a US or Canadian toll-free number — FCC rules (47 CFR §52.107) prohibit brokering. You reserve them through a Responsible Organization (RespOrg). We help you find available vanity numbers and connect you to licensed providers.
Can I buy a lucky mobile number?
In many countries, yes, from the carrier or an authorised auction — for example VIP numbers in China, the UAE and India. Rules vary; many carriers treat the number as a service contract you lease, not property you own.
How do licence plate auctions work?
Hong Kong's Transport Department, Dubai's RTA (often via Emirates Auction), the UK's DVLA and Singapore's LTA each run auctions or bidding windows. See our plate auctions guide.
How does the value estimator work?
It detects patterns buyers pay for — repeats, few distinct digits, ladders, mirrors, 8s, no 4s and shortness — and maps the score to an indicative band. It's a starting point, not an appraisal. See our methodology.
Do you store what I type into the tools?
No. Tools run entirely in your browser. Only forms you submit are sent to us.
What does the concierge cost?
Asking is free. If we source something, we quote any fee in writing before you commit.
Are lucky numbers actually lucky?
They're cultural beliefs, not guarantees. What's measurable is that they change what people will pay — that's what we document.

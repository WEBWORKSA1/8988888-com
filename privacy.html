Privacy Policy

Short, plain-language and honest.

Updated 2026-10-07 · 8988888 Research Desk

This policy explains what 8988888.com collects and why.

What we collect

  • Forms you submit (name, email, phone, request details) — used only to answer your request, and, for quote requests where you consent, shared with up to three matched providers.
  • Tool inputs — processed in your browser and never sent to us.
  • Cookies — a theme preference, your consent choice, and, if you accept, analytics (Google Analytics) and advertising cookies (Google AdSense).

Advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. If you choose “Essential only”, we request non-personalised ads.

Form processing

Form submissions are relayed by a third-party form service to our private inbox. We keep enquiries for up to 24 months, then delete them.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, PDPA) via the contact form. We don't sell personal data; sharing with providers happens only with your explicit consent on the quote form.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 (16 in the EEA), and contests are open to adults only.

Changes

We'll post updates here with a new date.

Numeric Domains: Why All-Digit .coms Sell and How to Value Them

All-digit domains are easy to type for speakers of any language — and in Chinese markets, the right digits carry meaning too.

Updated 2026-10-07 · 8988888 Research Desk

Why numeric domains work

  • Language-neutral: Pinyin and English spellings vary; digits don't.
  • Phone-friendly: easy to say and type on a numeric keypad.
  • Meaningful: 518, 168 and 888 carry Chinese meanings that brands want.

What drives value

  1. Length — shorter is exponentially rarer (2- and 3-digit .coms are fully taken).
  2. Digits — 8s and 6s up; 4s down in Chinese markets.
  3. Pattern — repeats (88888), mirrors (8998), and few distinct digits.
  4. Extension — .com leads; .cn and .net follow.

Buying and selling safely

Use a reputable marketplace or broker and an escrow service; verify the WHOIS/registrar before paying; transfer only after funds are secured. Score a domain in the value estimator (choose the Domain tab) or ask for a free valuation.

FAQ

Are 7-digit .coms valuable?
Most are inexpensive, but patterned, 4-free, 8-heavy ones command premiums with Chinese buyers.
How do I sell a numeric domain?
List it on a major marketplace with escrow, or use a broker; set a realistic price anchored to comparable sales.

Premium & VIP Mobile Numbers: Where They're Sold and What They Cost

From Chengdu to Dubai, carriers sell memorable numbers for real money. Here's how the market works.

Updated 2026-10-07 · 8988888 Research Desk

China

Carriers sell “靓号” (beautiful numbers) with premium fees or minimum spend plans, and numbers are sometimes auctioned. In 2003 Sichuan Airlines paid ¥2.33M for 8888-8888 in Chengdu; that auction of 100 numbers raised ¥7.13M. In 2020 a court-seized Beijing number with five consecutive 8s drew 5,000+ bids and sold for ¥2.25M (≈US$300,000).

UAE

Etisalat (e&) and du sell VIP numbers in tiers and at auction. Etisalat's 050-7777777 fetched AED 7.82M in 2014 — as a service contract, not ownership. At the 2023 Most Noble Numbers auction, 054-8888888 sold for AED 2.3M.

India

Operators sell “fancy” or VIP numbers through their own portals; a resale market of SIM-based VIP numbers exists, with transfers subject to KYC rules. Choose sellers that complete a proper ownership transfer in your name.

North America & Europe

Numbers are generally licensed to subscribers, not property. Memorable local numbers are found by searching provider inventory or porting with a business.

Buying checklist

  • Is the seller the carrier or an authorised auction?
  • Will the number be registered in your name (KYC)?
  • What's the monthly plan commitment?
  • Can you port it later?

FAQ

Do I own a VIP number I buy?
Usually you hold the right to use it under a service contract; ownership rules vary by country and carrier.

Short repeating numbers are status symbols, and charity auctions add prestige.

How to Choose a Business Phone Number (and System) That Wins Calls

A practical buyer's guide: number type, memorability, and the phone system behind it.

Updated 2026-10-07 · 8988888 Research Desk

Step 1: pick the number type

TypeBest forWatch out

Step 2: choose the phone system

Modern cloud (VoIP) systems typically include multiple numbers, auto attendant, voicemail-to-email, call recording, business SMS, mobile and desktop apps, video meetings and CRM integrations. Prices commonly run from about $15 to $75 per user per month depending on tier.

Buyer checklist

  • Can I keep (port) my current number?
  • Are toll-free minutes included?
  • Is there an AI receptionist or IVR?
  • Contract length and cancellation terms
  • Uptime SLA and support hours

FAQ

Should I use a toll-free or local number?
Use local for community trust, toll-free for national campaigns — many businesses run both on one system.
Can I keep my number when switching?
In most cases yes, through number porting.

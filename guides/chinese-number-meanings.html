Chinese Number Meanings 0–9: Lucky & Unlucky Numbers Explained

Every digit, its sound, its meaning — and the combinations that turn ordinary numbers into lucky (or unlucky) ones.

Updated 2026-10-07 · 8988888 Research Desk

Why sounds matter

Mandarin and Cantonese have many homophones, so a number can “sound like” a word. Over centuries these sound-alikes became shared cultural meanings — so strong that they move prices of homes, plates and phone numbers.

Digit by digit

#HanziMandarinCantoneseMeaning
0零língling4Wholeness, a clean start; neutral to good
1一yījat1Unity, “number one”; reads as 要 (must/will) in 168 and 518
2二èrji6Pairs, harmony — “good things come in pairs”; 易 easy in Cantonese
3三sānsaam1Life and growth — sounds like 生 (birth) in Cantonese
4四sìsei3Sounds like 死 death — widely avoided (tetraphobia)
5五wǔng5Five elements; 吾 “I/me”; can read as 无 “none”
6六liùluk6流 flow — 六六大顺 “everything goes smoothly”
7七qīcat1起 rise / 齐 together; 7th lunar month is Ghost Month — mixed
8八bābaat3发 prosper — the luckiest digit
9九jiǔgau2久 long-lasting; imperial number; weddings

Lucky combinations

  • 168 一路发 — prosperity all the way
  • 518 我要发 — I will prosper
  • 888 / 8888 — wealth upon wealth; prosperity in all four seasons
  • 89 / 98 — lasting prosperity
  • 666 — everything goes smoothly
  • 520 — I love you; 1314 — a lifetime
  • 28 — easy prosperity (Cantonese)

Unlucky combinations

  • 14 要死 “must die”, 74 气死 “furious to death”, 94 久死 “long death”, 54 吾死 “I die”
  • 250 二百五 — slang for a fool
  • 38 三八 — unflattering slang

Using this in business

Avoid 4 in prices, floor numbers, phone numbers and SKUs aimed at Chinese-speaking customers; prefer endings in 8, 88 or 168. Test any number in the luck scorer.

FAQ

What is the luckiest number in Chinese?
8, because 八 bā sounds like 发 fā, to prosper. Repeated 8s multiply the meaning.
What is the unluckiest number?
4, because 四 sì sounds like 死 sǐ, death.
